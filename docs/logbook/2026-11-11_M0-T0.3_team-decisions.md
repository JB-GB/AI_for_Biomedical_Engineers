| Question | Answer |

## Findings
- Public data for pre-training exists only for **general musculoskeletal US** (UMUD index: 14 datasets, ~76 k images, mostly muscle/tendon) and generic US (USF-MAE / OpenUS-46, ~370 k images). None has synovitis labels.
- Consequence: base model = staged transfer learning; clinical validity only after hospital fine-tuning.

## Decisions derived
- Staged data strategy S0–S3 (`00_feasibility.md §5`).
- Every model version records its training-data provenance; UI shows a warning banner for models without clinical data.
- 8 GB VRAM → backbones ≤ ~30 M params, mixed precision, batch 16–32 at 224–384 px.
