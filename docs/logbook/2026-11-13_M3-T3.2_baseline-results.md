## Results (test split: 198 images, 33 patients, never seen in training)
| Head | QWK | Accuracy | Balanced acc. | AUC (grade ≥ 2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GS | 0.970 | 0.965 | 0.973 | 1.000 |
| PD | 0.896 | 0.904 | 0.928 | 0.987 |
| **Combined** | **0.908** | **0.934** | **0.945** | **0.993** |

- Best epoch 12 of 20 (early stopping, patience 8); ~9 s/epoch on RTX 4070 Laptop; total < 4 min.
- Validation combined QWK 0.937.
- Combined confusion: 4/198 errors are large jumps to grade 3 (two 0→3, two 1→3).
  PD head predicted PD 3 for two PD-0 images → likely the out-of-synovium distractor vessel.
- QWK (0.908) < accuracy (0.934) because those few far errors are quadratically penalised.

## Interpretation
- The pipeline works end to end. Synthetic grades are learnable, as expected from a
  parametric generator; **these numbers are not clinical evidence** and must not be
  compared with the literature table except as a pipeline sanity check.
- PD is harder than GS (small spots, distractors) — consistent with the clinical difficulty.
