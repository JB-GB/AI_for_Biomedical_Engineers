## Context
Evaluate whether an AI model can pre-screen Rheumatoid Arthritis from
ultrasound images to reduce reliance on X-ray referral.

## Actions
- Literature scan (EULAR-OMERACT scoring, DL synovitis grading studies).
- Search for public RA ultrasound datasets (Zenodo, Mendeley, figshare, general web).

## Findings / Results
- Published DL models grade synovitis on the EULAR-OMERACT 0–3 scale with ~0.80–0.87 accuracy.
- US is more sensitive than X-ray for early inflammation; X-ray shows late damage.
- No clearly public, downloadable RA-synovitis US dataset was found.

## Blockers
- **Data access** is the critical blocker (owner: TBD, M1).

## Decisions
- Reframe the goal as *synovitis grading for triage / decision support*, not diagnosis.
- Labelling standard: EULAR-OMERACT (GS 0–3, PD 0–3, combined).
- Recommended stack: Option A (PyTorch + timm + Gradio), ONNX-ready. Pending team vote.
