# [2026-11-11] M1-T1.7 — Synthetic joint ultrasound generator (stage S2)

- **Author:** JB-GB
- **Type:** Result
- **Related:** `src/rausnet/data/synthetic.py`, `tests/test_synthetic.py`, `docs/paper/figures/synthetic_examples.png`

## Context
No clinical images are available yet. A parametric generator provides labelled
GS + PD images to build and test the full pipeline (stage S2).

## Actions
- Procedural renderer (numpy only) of dorsal longitudinal MCP 2–3 and wrist:
  skin, fibrillar extensor tendon, cortical bone with acoustic shadow, hyaline
  cartilage, joint gaps, hypoechoic synovial region, Rayleigh speckle, depth
  attenuation, log compression, random left/right orientation.
- GS grade controls synovium thickness, width and upper-surface shape
  (GS1 thin/narrow, GS2 flat, GS3 convex bulging).
- PD overlay inside a colour box: PD1 ≤ 3 spots, PD2 < 50 %, PD3 > 50 % of the synovium
  (verified per image, regenerated otherwise). 15 % of images include a vessel
  outside the synovium as a distractor.
- Patient-level disease activity → consistent grades per patient; 6 joints per patient.
- Every image has its own RNG `(seed, patient, joint, side)` → exact reproducibility.
- CLI: `rausnet-synth --out <dir> --patients 200 --seed 0`; `--preview <png>` for figures.

## Findings / Results
- First preview: synovium too thin and bones too shallow → thickness ranges increased
  (GS1 0.02–0.035, GS2 0.05–0.07, GS3 0.09–0.13 of depth), bones moved deeper.
- 200 patients → 1 200 images (256 px) in ~72 s on the laptop CPU.
- Combined-grade distribution (seed 0): 0: 402, 1: 344, 2: 344, **3: 110 (9 %)**.
- Patient-level split: train 816 / val 186 / test 198 images, no patient overlap.
- 48 unit tests pass (grade monotonicity, PD coverage rules, reproducibility).

## Blockers
None.

## Decisions
- Keep grade 3 imbalance (realistic); handle with class-weighted loss in M3.
- Masks are saved too (`masks/`) to allow a segmentation head later.

## Limitations (for the paper)
- Idealised anatomy and acoustics; no reverberation, anisotropy, or device-specific
  artefacts. Expect a large synthetic-to-real gap; results on S2 data only verify the pipeline.
